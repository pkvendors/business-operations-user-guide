
# 10. Frequently Asked Questions

## 1. How do I access BOMS?

Open the application URL and sign in
using your assigned credentials.

## 2. How do I add an employee?

Navigate to Employee Management and
select Add Employee.

## 3. Can I update employee information?

Authorized users can edit employee records.

## 4. How do I generate a report?

Navigate to Reports, select a report type,
enter the required criteria, and generate it.

## 5. Who should I contact for assistance?

Contact your system administrator.
