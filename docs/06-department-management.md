
# 6. Department Management

## 6.1 Overview

The Department Management module allows
authorized users to maintain department records.

## 6.2 Adding a Department

1. Open Department Management.
2. Select **Add Department**.
3. Enter the department name.
4. Enter the department description.
5. Assign a department manager.
6. Select **Save**.

## 6.3 Updating a Department

1. Locate the department.
2. Select the department record.
3. Click **Edit**.
4. Update the required information.
5. Click **Save**.

## 6.4 Expected Results

The department listing displays the updated
department information.
