
# 5. Employee Management

## 5.1 Overview

The Employee Management module allows authorized
users to maintain employee records.

## 5.2 Adding a New Employee

1. Log in to BOMS.
2. Select **Employee Management**.
3. Click **Add Employee**.
4. Enter the employee's information.
5. Review the information for accuracy.
6. Click **Save**.

## 5.3 Updating an Employee Record

1. Open the Employee Management module.
2. Search for the employee.
3. Select the employee record.
4. Click **Edit**.
5. Update the required information.
6. Click **Save**.

## 5.4 Searching for an Employee

1. Navigate to Employee Management.
2. Enter the employee's name or ID
   in the search field.
3. Click **Search**.
4. Review the matching results.
5. ![BOMS Employee Search](../images/employee-search.png)

## 5.5 Expected Results

After successfully saving an employee record,
the updated information should appear in the
employee listing.
